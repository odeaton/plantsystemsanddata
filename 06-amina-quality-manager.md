## What she said

- "Every lot is tested before release: melt flow, color, moisture for PA6. Two hours, six for automotive colors."
- "The results are in the Access database on the lab PC, by batch number. SAP does not know a lot is on hold. Corinne learns it at 8:30 or when we call."
- "When a lot fails, the order is produced again. That is a new order in the plan, usually urgent."
- "After a rushed changeover, the start-up material is off-spec. Forty-five percent of our scrap is start-up after a changeover."
