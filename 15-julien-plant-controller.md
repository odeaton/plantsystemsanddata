## What he said
- Contribution margin €447 per tonne, 26%; variable costs €1,274; full cost €1,608. "Never value a tonne at full cost. The fixed costs don't move."
- Scrap 4.2% of material input, net of regrind; material cost about €1,037 per tonne; disposal €0.15M a year.
- Overtime premium €0.9M, mostly Fridays and month-end.
- Inventory 78 days; COGS €123.8M; group carrying cost 18% a year.
- "The annex says changeovers are 11.5% of scheduled hours. The shift logs say less. The annex covers twelve months and all scheduled hours; the logs are extrusion only. I can tell you which one finance trusts."
- "I have never seen a gain from a project that I could find in my numbers. If you want me to sign one, I define the baseline, in week one."
