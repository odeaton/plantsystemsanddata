From Amina: lab release procedure QP-03, food-contact procedure QP-12, the BRC certificate, the UL file of Kerno, the PPAP file of Brisol, the 8D reports of 2024 to 2026.

- Red to green: 80 minutes, red shows in green for an hour.
- Conductive black 4040 to black 4010: 40 minutes, carbon residue.
- Never food-contact natural straight after conductive black. *QP-12*
- Never food-contact natural straight after black PA6. *QP-12*
- Never high-gloss white straight after red: pink tint.
- Food-contact natural 1020 only on BRC-certified lines M02, M03, M05, M07. *BRC certificate 2025*
- Standard white 2010 never on M06: PA6 residue yellows it.
- Duval never from M04: the Q1 color claim was traced to M04. *8D report Duval Q1 2026*
- Kerno only from the lines in its UL file: M03, M06. *UL file E-48211*
- Every lot waits two hours for lab release. *QP-03*
- PA6 lots wait four hours: moisture test. *QP-03*
- Food-contact natural waits three hours: migration check. *QP-12*
- Glass-filled waits 150 minutes: ash test for glass content. *QP-03*
- (Asked, not agreed) Duval prefers M03 for color stability.
