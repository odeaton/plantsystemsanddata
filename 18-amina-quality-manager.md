## What she said
- "About 45% of our scrap is start-up material after a changeover: the first minutes after a color or polymer change. The more changeovers, the more scrap."
- Other causes: off-spec color (about 25%), contamination after black (about 15%), the rest varied.
- Duval Home's claim in Q1 was a color mismatch on a batch produced after a rushed changeover.
- Lab results are in an Access database on the lab PC, linked to batch numbers, not to SAP orders.
- "If you reduce changeovers or make them cleaner, you reduce my scrap. That is the link nobody has measured."
