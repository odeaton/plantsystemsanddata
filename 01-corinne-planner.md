## What she said

- "The procedure? I know it. It describes 2019. The Friday plan is where I start, not where we end."
- **Friday.** "At 13:00 I export the open orders from SAP into my workbook. One tab per week. I sequence by hand: light to dark, natural, white, colors, black, then black runs into Saturday for the screw pull. Glass only on M01, M02, M05. PA6 on M03 and M06, never two PA6 starts within four hours because of the dryer. Four changeovers a shift at most. It takes me four to five hours. I send the PDF around 19:30."
- "I don't use the SAP planning board. It thinks every changeover is sixty minutes. I would spend my day fighting it."
- **Every morning.** "7:00, I read LineLog and Kevin's notes from the night. Something always moved. 7:30, I rewrite the next two days. 8:30, the morning meeting adds more: a maintenance window, a lot held by the lab, a priority from Hervé. 9:30, version two goes out. Sylvie keys it into SAP."
- **During the day.** "Stéphane's emails. Codra changes a date, Duval changes a quantity, a rush order comes in. Each time I look at the next 24 to 48 hours on one or two lines and I repair. I don't redo the week, I don't have time. Two to four times a day."
- "I never touch the order that is running or the next one. About four hours. You don't stop a line in the middle of a lot."
- **Priorities.** "Since February, Codra first, Hervé said so. Brisol has penalties in the contract, so Brisol second. Then the others. When two of them want the same line, I call Stéphane."
- "The CR-PLN form? I have not seen one since 2023."
- "By Wednesday, half of Friday's plan has moved. It is not a weekly plan. It is a daily plan with small repairs in between."
