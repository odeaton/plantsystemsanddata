## What he said

- "Retail customers change all the time: a date pulled in, a quantity up, a rush order on Friday for Tuesday. In April, four to six a day."
- "I send it to Corinne by email, or I walk to her desk if it is Codra. Then I wait. Sometimes an hour, usually the next morning."
- "I promise from SAP. SAP is yesterday's plan. So I add margin. That is why we quote nine to fourteen days when Codra wants seven."
- "If I could ask 'can we do 18 tonnes of PPW-2010 by Thursday?' and get an answer in fifteen minutes that holds, I would win orders."
- "Codra's rule: 95% on time, seven days, three months running before September."
