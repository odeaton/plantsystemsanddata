From Patrick's process walk and an export of the preventive plan in MaintPro.

- High-gloss white 2030 to white 2010: 35 minutes, melt filter change.
- On M04, black to colored: 150 minutes, the worn screw holds black.
- On M01, glass-filled to natural: 75 minutes, vented barrel cleans faster.
- On M04, never white straight after black: black specks from the worn screw.
- High-gloss white 2030 only on lines with the 40 µm melt filter: M01, M03, M05, M07.
- Glass fibre silo feeds M01, M02, M05: two glass starts at least 120 minutes apart.
- M04: at most two changeovers per shift, every purge needs a check.
- Black to white: Saturday 06:00 to 14:00, maintenance present.
- Black to white: also Wednesday 06:00 to 10:00, when maintenance is in for the weekly round.
- Preventive maintenance on M01 every Tuesday 06:00 to 09:00.
- Preventive maintenance on M02 every Wednesday 06:00 to 12:00.
- Preventive maintenance on M03 every Thursday 14:00 to 17:00.
- Preventive maintenance on M04 every Monday 06:00 to 08:00.
- Preventive maintenance on M05 every Tuesday 14:00 to 17:00.
- Preventive maintenance on M06 every Thursday 14:00 to 18:00.
- Preventive maintenance on M07 every Friday 06:00 to 08:00.
- M02 feeder calibration every Friday 14:00 to 15:00.
- On M02, glass-filled runs a further 10% slower: gearbox derating. *WO 2291*
- (Asked, not agreed) M05 at 95% in July and August: heat.
