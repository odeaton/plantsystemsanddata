Computed by the FDE team from twelve months of LineLog, checked with Julien and Patrick. The standard rate is 2.02 t per hour (2.30 ideal, 88% performance).

- Glass-filled runs at 85% of the standard rate: fibre feeding.
- PA6 runs at 90%: the dryer limits throughput.
- M07 runs at 92% on every product: smaller extruder.
- High-gloss white runs at 90%: melt filter.
- Conductive black runs at 92%.
