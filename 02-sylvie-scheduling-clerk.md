## What she said

- "Each version of Corinne's plan, I key into SAP: process orders, sequence, dates. Version two in the morning, then whatever changes in the afternoon. About twenty-five hours a week."
- "SAP is always behind. If Corinne changes at 15:00, it is in SAP the next morning. Stéphane promises from SAP, so he promises from yesterday."
- "I keep a notebook of every change: date, who asked, which order, what changed. Since February. Nobody asked me to. When something goes wrong, people come to me to know what happened."
- The FDE team built `data/change_log_w15-w18.csv` mostly from her notebook, cross-checked with Stéphane's mailbox, LineLog notes and the lab database. She checked the result.
- She asked what the project would change for her. We told her that the decision is not made and that she will hear it from Corinne first.
